# Reading and formatting notes

## The reading view

Press **Ctrl+R** (or **Read** at the top right of a note) to see it formatted: its name as a title, its fields in a box on the right, then its headings, lists, tables, pictures and links. Press it again to go back to editing.

**View > Open Notes in Reading View** opens every note this way.

You can edit right in the reading view:

- Click a paragraph, list or heading to change it. Click elsewhere, or press **Escape**, when you're done.
- Click a field in the box to change it, or the title to rename the note.
- Click a task box to tick it.
- Click under the note to add more text.

## Formatting without knowing Markdown

While you edit, the formatting toolbar above the note makes headings, **bold** (Ctrl+B) and *italic* (Ctrl+I) text, lists, task lists, links and pictures. Each button's tooltip shows the Markdown it writes, and pressing a button again takes the formatting away. **View > Formatting Toolbar** hides it.

If you know Markdown, just type it. Pressing **Enter** in a list carries it on, and pressing it on an empty item ends the list.

## Pictures

- **Edit > Insert Picture...** (Ctrl+Shift+K), or dropping a picture on the window, copies it into the campaign's `pictures` folder and puts it in the note.
- Pictures from the web work too: `![Map](https://example.com/map.png)`.
