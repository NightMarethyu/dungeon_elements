# Getting started

Dungeon Elements keeps a campaign as a folder of Markdown notes on your own drive. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and the same folder opens in Obsidian or any text editor.

## Make a campaign

1. On the start screen, choose **New Campaign...**
2. Give it a title and pick where to save it.
3. Under **Start from**, leave **Starter folders** picked to get folders for the campaign, sessions, players, quests, NPCs, factions, locations, lore and items, each with its own note fields and some with subfolders (like Enemies in NPCs and Settlements in Locations). Pick **Empty** for none, or one of your own [templates](guide:templates), like the folders and fields you use for one game system.
4. Choose **Create**.

To open a campaign you already have, choose **Open Campaign...**. It lists the campaigns you've opened or made, the latest first: click one to open it, or type part of its name to find it and press **Enter**. **×** takes a campaign off the list without touching its folder. **Browse for a Folder...** opens one that isn't listed yet.

## Find your way around

- The **file tree** on the left lists the campaign's folders and notes. Click a note to open it in a tab.
- The **menus** at the top hold everything the app can do:
  - **File** makes, saves and opens notes and campaigns.
  - **Edit** cuts, copies and pastes, inserts links and pictures, and finds notes.
  - **View** changes how notes are shown, which lists are open, and the theme.
  - **Campaign** has the walkthrough and the note types: the fields each kind of note has.
  - **Tables** has the dice roller and the roll tables.
  - **Maps** is greyed out for now, waiting for the map editor.
  - **Help** has these guides and the keyboard shortcuts.
- Hold **Alt** and press a menu's first letter to open it from the keyboard (**Alt+F** for File), then use the arrow keys and **Enter**.
- The **status bar** at the bottom says what just happened. If any notes have problems, a ⚠ there lists them.
- **Right-click** almost anything (a note, a folder, a tab, some text) for what you can do with it.

## Write your first note

1. Press **Ctrl+N** (or **File > New Note**) for a new note, or use the **+** next to a folder to make one of that folder's kind, like a new NPC.
2. Fill in its name and fields.
3. Press **Ctrl+S** to save it.
