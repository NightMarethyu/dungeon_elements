# Tracking quests

**View > Quests** (Ctrl+Shift+Q) shows the campaign's quests in the sidebar, in place of the file tree. You can also right-click a Quests folder and pick **Show Quests**. Close it with **✕** or **Escape** to see the file tree again.

## What it shows

- Every note in a folder of the Quest type, or a kind of it, grouped by its **Status**: Rumored, Available, Active and so on, in the order the Status choices are listed. Quests with no status are under **Other**.
- Complete, Failed and Abandoned start collapsed, so the quests still going are what you see first. Click a group's name to open or close it.
- Each quest shows how many of its Objectives are done, like `2/5`, with a bar under it. Click its name to open the note.

## Ticking off steps

Click the arrow beside a quest to see its steps, and click a step's box to tick it or untick it.

- If the quest is open in a tab, the tick goes into the tab and is saved with it, like any change you make there.
- If it isn't open, the tick is saved to the note straight away.

## Quest lines

A step that links to another quest, like `[[Find the Lost Map]]`, makes a quest line: a big quest made of smaller ones. The smaller quest shows under its step, with its own steps and progress, and isn't shown again at the top of the list.

A step that is another quest counts as done when its box is ticked, or when that quest's Status is Complete.
